Serving the community of the MRC de la Côte-de-Gaspé

The Royal Canadian Legion, Branch 59 of Gaspé, is a non-profit organization that exists to support and engage the citizens of our region, including youth, adults, families, seniors and veterans to enhance their standard of living and quality of life.

We also serve countless non-profits and special interest groups in our area, by providing them with services, programs and resources to facilitate the achievement of their mission and objectives.

About Us

The Royal Canadian Legion, Branch 59 in Gaspé, embodies the spirit of solidarity and service in our community.

As an organization rooted in our traditions and history, we provide a gathering place where people can come together, enjoy themselves, and build lasting connections.

Our activities and events are designed to promote inclusion, respect, and mutual support among our members and the residents of Gaspé. Additionally, our commitment to our veterans is evident through various initiatives that honor their dedication and bravery.

By working together, we strengthen the social fabric of our community and contribute to its ongoing prosperity.

Programs

The Royal Canadian Legion offers several concrete programs to support veterans and their families, including mentorship, education, and financial support programs. Additionally, it actively contributes to preserving Canada's military history and promoting values such as service, camaraderie, and solidarity.

01.

Veterans Support Program

The veterans support program aims to help veterans with their difficulties, whether physical or psychological, by directing them to the right resources or simply by listening to them.

02.

Poppy Fund Program

Poppy Funds are financial support for veterans. It is mainly used to feed and house veterans who do not have a lot of money.

03.

Descendants of Veterans Support Program

This program serves to encourage the children and grandchildren of a veteran to continue their studies by offering them one scholarship per year.

04.

Community Organization Support Program

The community organization support program has helped the community, for example by giving donations or even just giving them a free place to organize events.

05.

Seniors Engagement Program

The program helps seniors do everything essential to living in a healthy environment, such as housekeeping. The program also serves to provide rights to home care or just hospital care.

Services

The Canadian Legion in Gaspé offers a range of essential services to the local community and its members. These services include support for veterans and their families, including financial assistance, counseling, and referrals to appropriate resources. The Legion also organizes commemorative events and ceremonies to honor veterans and promote remembrance of their sacrifices. Additionally, it plays an active role in the social life of the region by organizing activities and initiatives aimed at strengthening community bonds and promoting patriotism and civic engagement. The Canadian Legion in Gaspé is an important pillar of local life, providing valuable support to veterans while contributing to the well-being and cohesion of the community.

01.

Hall Rental

The Legion in Gaspé rents out its space for events like meetings, parties, and weddings. They offer convenient amenities and support their mission of aiding veterans.

02.

War Museum

The Legion in Gaspé has a war museum showcasing local military history and honoring veterans' bravery. Visitors experience historical artifacts and stories of war heroes, deepening their understanding of conflicts' impact and preserving collective memory.

03.

Monthly Bingo

The Legion in Gaspé hosts a fun monthly bingo for the community, bringing people together for gaming and supporting important causes. It's a friendly atmosphere with prizes, contributing to Legion initiatives for veterans.

04.

Darts

The Canadian Legion in Gaspé hosts regular dart games, offering members and the local community a fun and competitive activity. Participants showcase their skills, enjoy camaraderie, and have the chance to meet new people while bonding over their love for the game. It's a great opportunity to unwind, socialize, and have a good time in a welcoming atmosphere.

05.

Billiards

The Legion in Gaspé has multiple pool tables for members and locals, offering diverse entertainment. It's a popular spot for skill improvement, friendly games, and tournaments, perfect for socializing and relaxation with quality equipment and a welcoming atmosphere.

06.

Support services for veterans and cadets

The Legion in Gaspé helps veterans, serving members of the Canadian Armed Forces and RCMP, and their families find the support they are entitled to. The branch connects them with Legion Service Officers for disability benefits and Veterans Affairs claims, and with emergency assistance through the Poppy Fund. It also supports local cadets, encouraging leadership, citizenship and the next generation of remembrance.
